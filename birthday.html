Day 2: Birthday

January 17, 2026
Scroll Down

Set my alarm for 6:49 so I’d be awake for my true Birthday at 6:50. Got a message from Mum but didn’t see anything from Dad. Back to sleep until 9. Still didn’t see anything from Dad and jokingly asked if he’d forgotten me and said even the dentist had messaged me - turned out he’d sent me an email at 6:50 but I had forgotten to check my emails, what a dope!

Had a shower and got ready. Pretty sure I wore this dress for my last 2 Birthdays haha

Off to pick up our hire car. Got an Uber at about 10:30 and arrived at the airport just before 11 - the trip cost about $50. The Uber driver drove into the main airport bit but we thought that was the wrong area and then we drove around to the car rental section - turns out we should have gotten out where we were first as we had to go to the counters inside the airport. There was no-one at the Enterprise counter when we got there, but we only had to wait a few minutes. Gave the extra details needed and got the keys to our MG3 Core. Luckily we didn’t have to pay for an extra driver as I was a member from back when we were in the UK. Decided not to get extra cover - it’s always a tough decision! It was like $40 extra a day which was a lot ($200). We’re already paying ~$600!

Already frazzled trying to get out of the carpark and went the wrong way and had to back back while another driver gave me a dirty look haha doesn’t he know it’s my Birthday!

First stop Maccas to see our favourite koala from the list, the Surf Lifesaver.

Then across to Settlement City shopping centre to get my Birthday Boost. Decided to try the new Melon Bloom. It was OK but I wouldn’t get it again. Pete wasn’t happy with how his two were mixed.

Drove back to John Downes Park to get the P plates off the Scooby so Pete will be able to drive the rental car as well. Saw a Turkey and an awesome Goanna.

Pete started driving to the next stop. There’s already a long list of things he hates about the car - seats too small, speedo in wrong spot, cruise control poop, mirrors don’t go back far enough, blinkers don’t stop blinking quick enough, gear shift doesn’t align, accelerator laggy, radio keeps turning on. On the plus side the car doesn’t turn off at the traffic lights like most new cars.

Realised that our intended next stop, the Koala souvenir shop, wasn’t open today (not open Fridays, Saturdays or Sundays - weird). So next stop now Ricardoes.

Arrived just before 1 and ordered a scone each and Pete got some chips. Thought the scones were a bit small but actually couldn’t have eaten more - wasted half the jam but ran out of scone and felt too much sweet overload to eat more anyway!

Had a look around the shop and bought a couple of things for Mum’s Birthday and some Moroccan spiced pumpkin soup for Pete and I to try.

Had a look at the hydroponic strawberry set up. Pete picked some strawberries before he realised they weren’t free haha (had to take them back inside to weigh them and pay for them) it will be interesting to see how much they cost! I was in the bathroom when Pete made the purchase but I think the total was $1.82.

Pete added another car complaint to the list - the key is too long haha

Next stop was Sovereign Place town centre, couldn’t really remember why but figured it was probably to see another koala statue. Decided to have a look in IGA first and was impressed how big the shop was and what a range - they even sell River Port Portello though it was sadly out of stock. Bought a couple of drinks and Pete bought me a Birthday duck. I will probably call her Cathie after Lake Cathie although it’s pronounced Cat-Eye so maybe not haha On the way out of IGA we found the koala!

Had a quick look in the knick-knack shop and got a Birthday card for Mum. Then on the road again.

Next stop Vickers Fudge Shop. Google led me astray but only by a few minutes and we got to see some nice new houses. Made it to the shop which was just after we’d first turned off the main road, and we’d both seen it but hadn’t realised. It was a bit of a strange shop - I was under the impression it was partly a factory where you could get tours and it would be bustling - turns out there was no-one else there but one worker, who couldn’t even be bothered to say Hi to us when we arrived haha We looked around for a bit and couldn’t decide what to buy but eventually got a freckle for me and some Kahlua fudge for Vicki. At least I think it’s Kahlua flavoured as it was spelled Kahula haha

Another car complaint from Pete - it smells bad (too sweet/clean).

Next we headed to Guulabaa “Place of Koala” to see the big koala. There were lots of people at the treetops park which was in the same area. Had a look in the koala gift shop and there were lots of nice things, but we didn’t buy anything. We admired the interesting sculpture.

Then onto our last planned stop which was Long Point vineyard and art gallery. Just before we arived we saw heaps of kangaroos in some front yards!

The vineyard was a lovely spot. They had taster options for $10-$15 but we couldn’t really partake as Pete can’t drink on his Ps. Perused the art. Decided to buy an orange liqueur - I was just going to buy it but she let me taste it first. Then Pete mentioned the ginger beer and I got to try that too - the first sample was towards the end of the bottle and it was very gingery and a bit flat. She opened another bottle and that was fizzier and lighter. Both were nice and we ended up getting a 6-pack. We had a quick look out the back and saw the sculptures1 and climbed the tree house.

Drove about 10mins back to our place and Pete dropped me off and I’ve been updating the journal while he went to try the NRMA again for the Scooby. I’ve just heard that it’s ‘fixed’ which I think means they’ve cut one of the belts so it won’t fling metal around the engine bay - it just means the A/C won’t work now, but that’s not the end of the world. Maybe we won’t have to worry about both driving home and then having to swap for the Delica and both having to drive back here to drop the hire car off on Wednesday. Will discuss with Pete when he’s back.

OK good news maybe - seems like the car will be OK for a bit longer, just without A/C. Pete came back to pick me up in the hire car, and we drove back to John Downes Park - I drove the hire car back and Pete drove the Scooby back to our cabin and it seemed OK. We’ll test him out a bit more tomorrow. These were some of the mangled bits -

Finally got around to opening my cards - one from the art gallery2, one from Mum and Dad, one from Pete and one from the Tikofts who also gave me a book and journal, nice!

Soon we’re going to walk about 15mins over to Rita’s Bistro for dinner - Mum and Dad are shouting. I just heard thunder though so we might regret the decision to walk haha

It was still sprinkling but we had a nice walk over to the Bistro. Saw the 2 tonne Big Bowl.

Pete ordered a curry and I was tossing up between a schnitzel sanga and the lamb shanks - ended up going with the latter. Everything was ready surprisingly quick which may not bode well haha Also got a Sex on the Beach frozen cocktail and Pete got an alcoholic ginger beer. Started eating at about 7:50. Pete’s curry smelled excellent, plus it came with poppadoms (one of which he generously shared with me). Some of my lamb shank was a bit fatty, but the good bits were delicious, along with the mash. The slushy cocktail was really good too. I was contemplating more but we noticed they were closing at 8:30 - so early!

Decided to try and make it to Woolworths before they closed at 9 - it was about 20mins walk away and we managed to make it with about 25mins to spare (we were pretty puffed though haha) Decided on Lamington fingers for my treat hehe walked the few mins home and only a few mins later it absolutely bucketed down so that was lucky!!

Enjoyed strawberries with the lamington fingers as my last snack of the day.

In other news, just wanted to mention something funny we’d seen earlier that day but couldn’t remember where - it was a sign advertising a few things for sale at a nearby shop/stall, including fudge and prawns - but the way it was written made us think of prawn fudge, a truly horrifying thought haha

1 - The Wanderers by Brad Jackson - Previously featured in Sculptures by the Sea at Bondi “The Wanderers are a travelling family of metal spheres. They pass by in silence. Their original destination remains a mystery to all but themselves. This work evolved from a study of body motion and movement, both implied and physical. Shapeless spheres are bought to life and given character and motion solely by the addition of realistic avian legs. The size of the work encourages both interaction and a sense of uncertainty regarding their presence and motive.”
2 - I got this card when we’d visited the Art Gallery of NSW with Mum and Dad in July, to see the Archibald’s. We also visited another exhibit called “Happy Birthday 2U2” by Hikoko Ito which included 366 mailboxes full of Birthday cards - the idea being you had to match a symbol to find the box for your Birthday, take a card made by one of your Birthday twins and leave a card you’d made. It was a fantastic idea that we really enjoyed doing together!
