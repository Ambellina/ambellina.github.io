Valenversary-2026

February 14, 2026
Scroll Down

The Valenversary celebrations started on Friday evening with a lovely card Pete left me (in the fridge haha)

Earlier that evening he’d mentioned that we’d be going somewhere on Valentine’s Day, and given me a cryptic clue to solve -

Pinch the tip, steal stones maybe. Sounds like a sure retreat. (4, 5)

The answer was Seal Rocks!

I got up at around 9:30-10 and started getting ready. Put on my nice dinosaur dress and even cut my nails in case there might be a kneely-surprise at some point in the day and I’d need to take a photo of my hand - spoiler alert there wasn’t haha

Couldn’t help but laugh at Looby who’d gotten stuck on the new garden netting.

We prepared some sandwiches for our lunch, and headed off at about 11:20.

Had a really nice drive through Waukivory through to Bulahdelah - we’d been to Waukivory, and a little further, for scenic drives in the past, but we’d never driven all the way through. Pete had driven the reverse direction a couple of times in the dark when the Bucketts Way had been closed. And about 2 years prior we’d driven the reverse after doing a property viewing with Gary Ferris before he took us to see our eventual home in Tugrabakh. This time we got to take our time and look at the surroundings, which was nice. Didn’t see many other cars.

After Bulahdelah we joined the highway only for a few minutes before taking the Seal Rocks turn off. The next lot of scenery was also delightful and now had more of a rainforest feel in parts. Eventually arrived at Seal Rocks in a bit under 2 hours.

Parked at ‘Number One Beach’ and saw some Eastern Yellow Robins that seemed quite tame. Pete got some nice shots.

Found a picnic shelter and enjoyed our ham, cheese and tomato sandwiches. It was warm, but not unpleasant, with a slight breeze. Pete wouldn’t pause scoffing his face to take a photo haha

Tried to decide whether to go for a walk or a swim. Debated whether I could be bothered changing into my swimmers and putting all the sunscreen on, but decided it was a good opportunity (warm, but not stinking hot). Put our picnic bits back in the car, got changed and lathered up, then headed down onto the sand. Found a lovely sheltered cove and made our way in - the water was actually perfect. Pete had a great time spotting crabs on the rocks.

We had a bit of a swim, then walked around the point - which seems to be called Hedgehog Island hehe - it was a bit rough on the feet but had a nice view around to the next Bay. Headed back to our little cove to cool off again. Swam for a bit more then got out to walk along the beach a bit.

Went for a drive around to the next Bay and Pete spotted a pirate ship haha

Continued driving up towards the lighthouse - it was a ~700m walk from the parking area and we debated if we should go or not (still in our wet clothes after swimming) but very glad we did! The first part of the walk was fairly sheltered and flat and very peaceful. We saw lots of little birds.

Nearing the lighthouse, the trail opened up and we saw some interesting rock formations and water as we spied the lighthouse at the top of the hill.

There were some nice views and some questionable views as we reached the top of the first hill haha

There was a nice grassy area with more great views.

Along the way Pete spotted more birds.

Next we headed up to the lighthouse - it’s hard to tell from the picture but it was very steep!

More great views at the top.

As we headed back down from the lighthouse, I thought I spotted something out in the water (dolphin?) but then we got distracted by an eagle.

We headed back to the nice grassy viewpoint but couldn’t spot the sea creature again.
It started to pour. Pete found another nice bird.

We got a bit wet on the walk back, but it was quite refreshing.

Drove a short distance to a little shop which had some interesting art out the front.

I bought a Honeycomb Maxibon icecream (I don’t think I’d had a Maxibon before and was a bit unsure how to eat it haha) Pete bought a Weis Bar and a Ginger Beer, and we both got a Killer Python. When we opened the paper bag with the Killer ‘Python’ it was more like a Killer ‘Skink’1 haha

Started the drive home and our first stop was Bulahdelah. It rained most of the way. Pete was starting to feel a bit sleepy, so we decided to swap the driving. Bought a couple of things in IGA and considered getting something at the cafe, but the Breakfast Pocket I had my eye on wasn’t available at that time, so we headed on home.

Quite a nice drive back towards Waukivory again. Didn’t have any cars behind me until right near the end. Stopped at Ashmar Farmstay which had a little stall out the front and got some Dwarf Sweet Pea seeds for $2 and a small bunch of red bush roses for $5.

As we were nearing home and debating what to have for dinner, we thought we’d try the Thai place in Gloucester again. We’d only had it once, nearly 2 years previously, and were very disappointed in all the meals and thought we’d never go back. But we’d recently seen a lot of people recommending it on Facebook, so thought we’d give it another go. It would either be good, or we’d get a laugh about our bad decision making haha It was certainly busy when we arrived - seemed like a good sign, though we weren’t sure if it was always busy or just due to a Valentine’s crowd. It was going to be a 30min wait for our order so Pete very chivalrously offered to drive me home so I could shower and wash my hair and get warm again. As it turned out, we were pleasantly surprised by dinner. I had ordered some curry puffs which were nice - I’d also ordered sweet and sour pork which was nothing to write home about but OK. But Pete’s meals were all good, especially the Panang curry which was delicious - he generously shared some with me but I wish he’d gone halves it was so good2.

All-in-all, a very successful day ♥︎

1 - It must have been a while since I’d had a true Killer Python, as it looks like they’d downsized them way back in 2014!! As part of a global initiative to reduce sugar and calorie intake, Nestlé reduced the weight from 47g to 24g and the length from 32.5cm to 21cm, cutting the calorie count from 630kJ to 336 kJ.


2 - I couldn’t stop thinking about it all week and decided we’d have Thai for dinner the following Saturday so I could get my own haha
