Tugrabakh - 2 Years

March 11, 2026
Scroll Down

March 11 marked our 2-year anniversary since we purchased our home in Tugrabakh. We are fairly settled into ‘country’ living now, though do occasionally miss the convenience that came with living in a big city - like Deliveroo, overnight Amazon deliveries and Hello Fresh!

It’s been a big year - we finalised the plans for our new house, got council approval, got loan approval, and work on the house site got underway on the 23rd March. There have been a few spanners and sleepless nights getting to this point, but everything seems on track now. The current timeline has the build finishing in October, but we are realistic about how these things always take longer (and cost more!) than expected. We are lucky that we have the cottage to live in, and are just building up the hill, so we don’t really have any time pressure to move in asap. But it’s exciting to think we could be in the new house by Christmas!
