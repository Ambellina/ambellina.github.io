Day 6 - Wednesday 11th Feb

February 11, 2026
Scroll Down

Happy Birthday Mum!!

Messaged Mum at about quarter to 7 to see if she wanted to go to breakfast - everyone was feeling a bit tired so we decided on brunch and to meet at Roadies at 11. Except Pete who hadn’t come to bed yet and would sleep in.

I managed to get ready a little early so I could stop at Woolies first to get Mum some flowers and some Red Bull for Pete.

Met at Roadies and got a table inside. Mum ordered a Breakfast Bagel and English Breakfast Tea, Dad ordered a Bacon and Egg Roll and a Strawberry Milkshake and I ordered Eggs Avocado and a Grape Slushie. It was all nice.

It wasn’t too busy when we arrived but then every man and his dog seemed to turn up. I got back in line to order a Bacon and Egg roll take away to take home to Pete.

Had a quick drive out to Dangar Rd to see the houses with dark exteriors that we’d discussed the other day. Then drove home and caught up with Pete. Watched some Winter Olympics and gave Mum her present - Mangoes and Cream tea, which actually I got Dad to buy and I forgot to pay him back, a Strawberry sauce and a Mango Butter I got from Ricardoes Tomatoes when Pete and I were up near Port Macquarie recently.

Watched more Squid Game. Later, Dad and I drove to Woolies to get some supplies for dinner. Back home and we finished Season 2 of Squid Game.

Dad, Pete and I walked up the driveway. Dad tried to entice some cows with an impressive bunch of grass, but they weren’t interested.

We then walked down to take the bin out and saw the falcon again. Also saw neighbour Pete and spoke to him briefly.

When we returned, we were excited to see the cows were now on the other side of the house and my rose bush was starting to flower again.

I snuck up on Dad giving Looby some words of encouragement hehe

Pete got a nice photo of the Jag in the sunset.

More Squid Game then made burgers and chips for dinner. The patties were the rissoles Mum and Dad bought in Stroud the other day. I was a bit unsure about them as they had peas in them haha but actually they tasted pretty good.

Sad to say goodbye but had a great 6 days!

As I was writing the journal in bed I heard a bang and then Pete turned all the lights on - a small bat had flown into the flyscreen in the office, then kept banging against the roof on the porch for a minute or so before finding his way off into the night. We sure had a lot of great guests in the last week!!

~Epilogue~

We had a beautiful misty sunrise on Thursday.

Mum and Dad arrived home safely around midday. They had stopped at Hattam Farm for some produce, which looked delicious. The flowers survived the journey and looked even better than when I bought them, which was a happy surprise!
