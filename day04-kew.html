Day 04: Kew Christmas

November 29, 2023
Scroll Down

More packing was done on Wednesday. Amber and I walked to Tooting to buy chicken rolls for lunch. The shop is owned by a nice Italian man. A nice compact one man shop. Lots of different types of bread and pastries for sale. His coffee machine was broken. His rolls were very nice but a bit expensive. He told us that he had once won a trip to Australia to look at the wine regions. He visited N.S.W., Victoria and South Australia. He had some nice bottles of wine for sale in his shop.

That night we went to Kew to view the Christmas lights display. We had a time slot at Kew of 8 p.m. Got the bus to Tooting Broadway. My Oyster Card wouldn’t work. Don’t know why as it had worked the day before. It still had money on it. We got three trains and eventually alighted at Richmond Station. Walked to the Kew Garden entrance. Took a lot longer than the 15 minutes that the sign had said. It was a pleasant stroll nevertheless despite the temperature.

We looked in the gift shop first. A lot of excellent items: seeds, books, cards, tea towels, beanies, scarves etc. We then walked around the Christmas light display. Amber and Pete had been in previous years but thought that these lights / displays were the best yet. There were really impressive large scale displays and some truly inspiring lights and accompanying music displays. The crowds, whilst still a good size, were much better mid week than they usually are at weekends. The temperature was a chilly 3 degrees but we had a great time. Towards the end, Pete rushed back to the gift shop before it closed to buy a present for his mother. Unfortunately, on the way he lost his scarf. He messaged Amber to keep a look out for it. Luckily, some kind person had found it and hung it up on the fence which made it easier for us to find.

A fantastic night.

As it was chilly, we decided to catch the bus back to Richmond Station rather than walk. We caught the train to Clapham Junction and then another bus. Noodles for tea.
