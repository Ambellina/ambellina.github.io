Day 5 - Tuesday 10th Feb

February 10, 2026
Scroll Down

Got up just before 10. Had tried to sleep in a little as Pete had to work the night before. We planned for Mum and Dad to pick us up at about 11:30. Had my shower and Pete was already awake so revised the pick up to 11:15. As usual he ended up being ready before me and my poops held everything up. We left at about 11:30.

Headed to Forster via Krambach and Nabiac. About 20mins out decided to give Uncle Trent a call in case he was free for lunch, but unfortunately he already had plans.

Drove into town and found a fish and chip shop but as we looked for parking we then decided it would be best to find our lunch spot first and drop Mum there to wait while we ordered the food. Headed up to the beach and found a cafe right on the water with parking and shaded seats so realised that was the jackpot option. This is what we ordered, plus a Pepsi Max I had to order inside as they didn’t have soft drinks on the app menu -

The meals were all very nice, and we had a nice view - the only downside was we felt quite warm with the sun reflecting off the nearby sand.

Afterwards Pete went to the toilet, then Mum and I decided to go as well and meet the others back at the car. While I was weeing I saw Pete had sent me a photo of Uncle Trent and Bea!!

We headed back to the table and had a quick chat - how nice to have run into each other! They said they might visit in the next few weeks.

Next we headed to PGH Bricks and Pavers to see some brick samples as we still hadn’t decided on what we wanted for the new house. It was a very successful trip as we’re now much more confident about the roof colour (Colorbond - Deep Ocean) and found two brick colours we liked (Red Gum and St George). The ladies were very helpful and explained some other things including about the grouting styles.

Drove around for a bit to try and get cool in the car A/C after having been out in the sun. Saw a nice turtle mural.

Had a couple of stops to look at views and debated whether to go for a swim, but eventually decided to head home.

Stopped at Nabiac so I could get Mum a Birthday scratchie1 seeing the local IGA newsagents has been closed due to water damage. Also stopped at the bakery and got an apple pie, lamgington, vanilla slice and chocolate muffin for us to share later.

Dad drove to Krambach then we swapped and I drove the last bit home. It was still very hot when we got home and we were glad to get inside.

Started Season 2 of Squid Game. After the first episode Pete decided to go for a nap. Dad and I had a look in the garden and admired the two small pumpkins he’d discovered that morning.

Watched S02E02 of Squid Game then Mum, Dad and I spent some time outside - much nicer now with a breeze. Dad found another pumpkin.

Mum headed back inside and Dad and I walked up the driveway to see if we could see the cows. Heard a moo on the way but it was Pete haha

Prepared some nibblies for dinner, along with the bakery treats. Watched two more episodes of Squid Game.

As Mum and Dad were leaving, we looked for BunBun and I excitedly spotted him in the front yard. Pete followed him and herded him around the house back our way and we got a good view of him hopping towards the cattle yard, so cute!

Just as I was getting ready for bed, I was surprised by a gorgeous froggy guest just inside the mudroom door!

1 - In my bedroom in Cherrybrook I’d recently found an envelope with various groupings of money - mostly $50 from Nana and Poppy, for lots of Birthdays in my 20s - I always struggle to spend money like that as I feel like I have to buy something memorable and I can never think of anything good enough. I can hear Pete in my head telling me that I should have spent it because it’s not worth as much if I hold on to it - he’s right, a $50 from twenty years ago would apparently now only have 62% of the purchasing power it had then. Or another way of looking at it is it will now cost ~$80 to buy now what I could have bought with $50 back then. Anyway, I’d also found some money labelled “Mum Lucky $50” so I decided to buy the scratchie with that money. Unfortunately it wasn’t lucky when Mum scratched it on her Birthday :( Maybe we’ll have more luck with the remaining money!
