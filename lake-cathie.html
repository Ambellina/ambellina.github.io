Day 3: Lake Cathie

January 18, 2026
Scroll Down

It was rainy in the morning. Stayed in bed and responded to some Birthday messages. Wanted to go for a dip so waited until the weather cleared a bit before 11. It was still a bit breezy and cool, but we thought we’d chance our luck. It was only about a 7min walk to the beach so very handy. The waves were crazy so no way we were going in properly, but could get to about butt height comfortably. I thought it would be freezing but it was actually quite pleasant! Pete didn’t seem too convinced but had a little paddle too. We walked around to the inlet and back home that way. Saw a couple of pelicans and people fishing.

Had a shower and set off for today’s adventures. Pete is sad the A/C is broken.

First stop was Windmill Hill Reserve to see the sculpture ‘Folly’ by Rick Reynolds - it represents the wooden shaft and stone grinding wheels of the windmill which once stood on the site.

Then off to see the Big Lizard. Happened across another koala statue on the way, this one was called ‘Starry Soul’.

Lots of homeless and day-drinkers at the park with the big lizard1.

Next stop was the Big Globe, but from a distance it didn’t look too impressive and as it was on a big intersection we decided not to stop. But I got a photo out the window as we turned the corner.

A short drive out to Settlement Point. Saw the ferry that goes across to the North Shore. In the Hastings River across to Pelican Island, we saw some white birds diving into the water, and some Sandpipers. Pete got some great shots.

While Pete was taking photos, I walked around to see the ferry come in. It was $8 per trip which only takes a few minutes. But at least you can get resident passes of some sort that would make things more reasonable.

Had a look at the cafe - thought I might be able to get something pre-made and takeaway, but there was a sign at the door to wait to be seated and their kitchen was closing in about 15mins so I decided against it. Pete came to meet me and we had a look in the bait shop - interesting they kept all their lollies in the fridge. Makes sense for chocolate but a bit weird for marshmallows and snakes hehe I got a Solero and Pete got a Mars Bar - he reckoned his cost $2.50 and my ice cream must have been $6!! Damn. Enjoyed it in the cool breeze but some of it melted too quick and dripped on my skirt, booooo hehe

Grookey Community Day (a Pokémon event) started at 2pm. We caught a few in the park then headed on our way again. Next stop was Spotlight to see if I could use my Birthday voucher - a $10 voucher if I spent $20 - they had a lot more things there than I thought they would, but nothing really took my fancy.

Next we headed back to Settlement City as Pete felt like a kebab - we had a quick look around the shops then headed back to the food court but it seemed the kebab place had just shut at 3pm. Decided to try and use my $50 Red Rooster voucher that I’d won from the Roosters last August. The food was OK but we probably wouldn’t get it again unless it was free again hehe

Last stop was at Bonny Hills Nursery - sooooo many great things and well priced! Pete bought me a plant and we also got some seeds - Watermelon, Carrots and a Red/White Strawberry mix. Some nice pots were already cheap and then half price, but I thought I better save my money. I could have bought them with the money Mum gave me nearly a year ago but I forgot :(

Drove around a couple of side streets on the way home and saw some nice houses and gardens and one epic letterbox!

Back home now - 5:30 and having a bit of a rest. Had wanted to get fish and chips for dinner but now I’m not sure I can be bothered haha

Researched a few options and decided on The Chip Shop. I only wanted 1 piece of Hoki. Still full from lunch. Pete got two pieces, large chips and a chocolate milkshake. The fish was much bigger than we thought it would be, it was nice but we only ate half. The chips were pretty average though. Ah well. Lamington and strawberries for dessert :D

1 - While looking for more info about the lizard, I found this funny article which aptly sums things up.
