Build Week 05

Mon 20 - Sun 26 April

April 26, 2026
Scroll Down

Pete spoke to Lukas early Monday, before he came to bed, about whether we might be able to get some extra drainage for the North side kitchen benches.

Found out that the Gloucester Markets have been cancelled for the time being. Someone said the “committee ran it into the ground I think, in debt and no-one wants to take it on, along with the NYE night” - what a shame.

Penny organised for us to have a site meeting on Wednesday to discuss a few points.

Pete and I finally got around to watering the natives again.

Nothing too much seemed to happen up at the house site today, except some re-fencing to enclose the tank site.

After dark, I noticed a Bogong Moth in the mudroom. Pete caught him and put him outside, but he came straight back under the door. After the second time taking him outside we understood why he didn’t want to be out there :(

I made some pastry-pizzas for dinner - first time in the air fryer was much quicker than the oven.

On one of my midnight bathroom trips a mouse darted into my foot! Well it was either a big mouse, or a small rat - he was going too fast for me to really tell!

The camera on the shed caught a heap of Chippers out for an early adventure on Tuesday.

Marty from Newcastle BYD called and said we’d be able to pick the Shark up at 3pm on Thursday next week - woohoo! It was going to be a bit of a hassle for me going mid-week, so I booked a 1/2 day to make things a bit easier. I’d probably work a couple of hours in the morning, and then a couple of hours in the evening - but I cancelled my 8pm call so I wouldn’t be stressed in case I couldn’t be back in time. We thought about staying in Newcastle but it’s a bit harder now Liam and Issy have moved back home and we don’t have a separate room for our ‘office’ now, and I didn’t want to have to take more time off work at short notice, especially as Monday was already a holiday. Hopefully we can pick up the car and still have enough time to run a few errands - like visiting a tile place, getting a kitchen quote, and Pete is desperate for a haircut!! Marty said our new tyres might not be available in time, and I asked whether it would be better to wait but he said they could be 6 weeks! So we’ll probably have to go for another trip in the near future. On the plus side, it will hopefully mean we can keep the first set of tyres (for future DIY projects TBC) rather than them just being swapped out.

Had my break at about 2pm and checked on the garden beds. Noticed the capsicum plant was really loaded!

Also so excited to discover some watermelons!!

We had so many cucumbers, we decided to pick some to take to the share shop.

We swapped our cucumbers for a tomato plant and a couple of wild iris.

In other news, IGA is STILL closed. The bottle-o and newsagents part has been closed since early Feb when a downpour flooded the building - it seems they’ve had a lot of trouble completing the repairs due to the presence of asbestos. The grocery side had re-opened but was now closed again for a week or so - presumably so they could finish things off. It’s unfortunate timing for them though, to be closed across the long weekend.

Back home, I tried to plant the irises but I didn’t do a very good job and they’ve already fallen over.

I got a nice picture of Pete in the sunlight - how good are those hills! (Side note - he really does need a haircut hehe)

In the evening, Pete cashed out his UK stocks so we could use the money towards the house. He tried to send it to my UK bank account so I could forward it through a money exchange account (Wise) that I already had set up - but annoyingly Barclays thought it might have been a scam and temporarily blocked both our accounts! Pete had to put money on his UK SIM so he could call them and sort it out.

It finally rained on Wednesday morning, yay!

Penny and Lukas came for the site meeting at about 1:30. We met up at the house site first to discuss some adjustments - including Pete’s request for some extra drainage for the kitchen benches which the plumber was apparently a bit offside about, but Lukas said he’d sort him out hehe We also spoke about some changes in the master ensuite as we were a bit concerned about the shower size. We headed back to the shed for the rest of the meeting as it was a bit warmer out of the wind. Full agenda below for reference1.

3 trucks of gravel arrived later that afternoon. There was a nice rainbow when Lukas was leaving!

Pete took his daily drone shot. I wanted to run up to the house site but I temporarily busted my ankle and couldn’t put any weight on it, so Pete piggy-backed me all the way there and all the way back!!

Just after we got back to the cottage, another truck arrived with some wire mesh. It was dark as he was leaving!

We had the most amazing Mexican wraps for dinner - one of the best meals we’ve ever made!

On Thursday we had a couple of new vehicles on site (P platers!) and a skip bin was delivered.

About 2:30 we headed out in the buggy - Pete found a new way across the creek which was good. We went to check on the natives - it had been raining a little but we watered our favourites. We noticed that a few of the guards had been done wrong (wood stakes inside-outside instead of outside-inside) - so I guess I wasn’t the only one who didn’t know the rules hehe

Went to visit the cows, then found another new way across the creek. Pete took us to the old tree and said I had to pose.

Back at the cottage I trimmed some plants, replanted the irises, watered the natives on the verandah and discovered one of the grevillea had flowered!

By that time, the workers had left for the day, so we walked up to the house site to see the latest. The gravel dust had been spread out and some formwork done.

On the walk back, there was a really lovely view of all the autumn colours and the cows walking in the distance - I couldn’t get a good photo as I only had my phone and they were so far away, but the shots still turned out quite interesting.

When reviewing the Friday morning footage, I noticed a new car driving on the grass - bloody P platers!! In hindsight I don’t think they knew where they were going and were initially going to pull over, but then decided to keep heading up the driveway.

Later I spotted a doggo!!

At lunch we headed in to town as Pete said there was a delivery I needed to pick up from the Post Office. He was up to something but I didn’t yet know what. It turns out he surprised me with a new pair of Merry People boots, yay!

Parked in the main street and saw one of the friendly workers from Woolies who advised us to come to the deli later in the evening as everything would be 50% off. First we headed to the Butcher - finally they had the Hawaiian sausages again!! Decided to go to Woolies next rather than wasting more petrol coming back later. The place was packed with bloody out-of-towners haha

Went for a little drive down Relfs road so we could relax again hehe it’s always nice seeing the goats and cows on the way, and then the river at the end. The autumn colours in the grasses were really nice and we saw heaps of ducks! Also saw a rainbow right in the paddock as we drove back.

Tried my new boots on when I got back - they’re so bright but I love them!!

Headed up to the house site at about 3:30. Some of the EPS had gone in now.

Hawaiian sausages, bacon and chippies for dinner - we won’t make the mistake of buying Woolies frozen chips again haha I thought “they’re just potatoes, how could they get that wrong” but they found a way.

Had a nice walk up to the house site on Saturday morning, it was so peaceful. Then down the driveway to admire the autumn colours. We’re very hopeful we won’t have to remove the lower trees when we do the driveway upgrade.

Around 1:30 we headed for a putt to visit the cows. It was nice just sitting in the buggy watching them. We tried to interest them in some music but they weren’t too keen this time.

We took the buggy down to the bridge to investigate the hives we’d seen last time - seems like they might belong to wasps rather than bees.

It was quite easy to get across the creek and we decided to have a wander and see how far we could get. We’d never really been able to walk on the far side of the creek before, and we had a great adventure going up and down and through, all the way to the corner and around to where the squash is growing. I felt like a kid again climbing up the steep bits and ducking under low branches. It was really cool seeing everything from a different angle.

We even got to see some of Don’s cows (on David and Kerry’s block).

Next we investigated the squash - one in particular was coming along quite nicely, though we’re still unsure what it’s going to turn out like!

Headed back to the cottage and it absolutely poured a few minutes later, so that was good timing! I started watching the coverage of the annual Roosters v St. George ANZAC Day match, a great tradition. Lots of good memories although we didn’t always have a great record - we lost 6 in a row before I went to the UK! Our record had been much better in recent years though, and we put on an absolute blinder today!

After the match I spent some time trying to organise my finances into the various new accounts we had set up. I accidentally put $10K into the loan account instead of one of the offset accounts, and we might not be able to get it back now (we haven’t lost it, it just means we can’t use it later if we need to). I’ll try to find out more when the banks are open again on Tuesday.

Spent a couple of hours after that reformatting Pete’s budget planning spreadsheet into a new layout with columns for each of the 4 main accounts so we can track our expected money in and out across the next year. Pete had diligently planned out a lot of the upcoming purchases - researching likely costs and working out when we’d need things by. Now we were just doing a little extra planning to make sure we’d have enough money to cover everything in the right timeframe. So far things seem on track.

It was after 9:30 when I finished updating the sheet and I had forgotten to have dinner. I was too tired to be bothered now so I just went to bed haha

On Sunday I had my daily walk around the house and said good morning to Willie.

I continued with the journal - almost only a week behind now! haha

Around 1pm we headed in to meet Brenda to get the booties I ordered for Mum. We met in the Woolies overflow carpark - the place was packed with long-weekend tourists. Brenda said there was no bread or roasts left. We went in to have a look and there was indeed no bread! Crazy. We left without buying anything.

Went for a drive down Relfs Rd to the river but there weren’t many birds today, except for a big flock of cow birds that circled a couple of times.

We had a look down Irrawang Rd too - we saw a couple of guys playing golf in their front yard. We also spotted our plumber’s home - we recognised his truck, which has the same sign as the one he put on our front fence.

On the way home, as we were driving along the Bucketts, a hawk or falcon (I can never remember which) was flying really low and flew alongside us for a little. It flew right near the house where our goat friends used to live. We pulled over so Pete could try and get a few photos, as he had his good camera in the back.

Next we stopped on the Bucketts/Bundook corner to see if I could spot any koalas, but no luck. Drove a little further down Bundook Rd past the bridge, to see what we could find - we stopped on the dirt road and Pete used his bird app to hear what was nearby.

Had lunch and did some more journalling - finally starting to catch up!
