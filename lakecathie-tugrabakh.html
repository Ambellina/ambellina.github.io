Day 4: Lake Cathie -> Tugrabakh

January 19, 2026
Scroll Down

I’d set my alarm for 8 so we had plenty of time to get ready before the 10am checkout. I ended up getting up at about 7:40 - so early!

A guy was blocking the exit while he took forever to pack his 4WD. He had a spot where he could have parked next to his cabin out of the way but I guess the 2m difference was slightly less convenient. I felt bad, but I asked if he could move his car for a moment so I could swap our cars (the hire car was next to the cabin, but the Scooby that we needed to pack was out on the road). Had to rush and swap them while he waited in his car slightly out of the way and as soon as I was back he blocked the road again haha

We got the house clean and the Scooby packed and headed off at about 9:40 - old mate had his boat attached now and was blocking things even more haha had to get him to move again so Pete could drive out. We headed on our way - Pete in the Scooby and me following in the hire car.

First stop was the Koala shop that we couldn’t visit on Sat as initially planned. It was in a bit of an industrial area which I wasn’t expecting. I thought it would be one of the main tourist shops in the middle of town. It had lots of cool stuff but we just got a couple of magnets and browsed the statues. I had to dash off as my belly was grumbly. Luckily there was a nearby cafe where I could sneak into the loo while Pete got a milkshake.

Next stop was to get petrol for the hire car before we dropped it back. Good thing Pete remembered as it hadn’t crossed my mind at all hehe Petrol splashed all out on me and the car before the nozzle clicked - another stupid thing about the car to add to our long list of complaints!

We got to the airport and I eventually found a suitable spot (it had to be within a certain range of numbers but they were all worn and I could hardly read them). Had to take lots of pictures of the car and then we walked over to the terminal to hand in the keys. I asked if we could get any discount seeing we brought it back a little over 2 days early - the man said he’d try, but as it was booked through the website it probably wouldn’t be possible1.

We called Collins W Collins to see if they could start next steps on our house plans now they’re back in the office.

Next stop was at the Supa IGA to see if they’d restocked the River Port Portello - no luck, and a young man checked out the back as well but none there either, shame.

Stopped at Kew to get petrol for Scooby and hold a big axe.

Nothing much exiting on the rest of the drive home. Though it was nice to see everything looking greener. And the cows were right up near the fence as we were driving past. Got home at about 1:20 and Pete and Looby were almost straight back to work haha I’ve unpacked most of the things and having a sit now.

Later we went into town. First up to Kia Ora just for a look and to see if we could see Huey. Didn’t spot him but saw two big eagles floating around, they were beautiful. Luckily Pete had his camera.

Got some groceries and headed home. Opened my card from Laura, which had been waiting in the letterbox. Heard some new chirps and found two Willie bub-bubs in the tree out the front - they can fly but can’t have been out of the nest for long. Had a nice little walk down to the creek.

All up, a great Birthday weekend!

1 - Never did get that discount/refund! And we waited over 2 weeks to get our security deposit back that we were supposed to get back within 48hrs! So not sure what’s going on there. At the date of writing (now March 3rd) the notice is still there, so it’s probably lucky we got the security deposit back at all!
