Tugrabakh - Early Days

April 28, 2024
Scroll Down

Originally posted on Facebook - 28 April 2024

After 10 years in the UK, some of you may have heard that Pete and I have moved back to Australia. Well we’re excited to share some more big news: we have recently purchased a property in Tugrabakh, just outside Gloucester. We finalised settlement on a beautiful 100 acre block on the 11th of March.

We didn’t think much of the house at first, but instantly fell in love with the land. We have been coming up on weekends (with a lot of help from family!) to do a lot of cleaning and get the house in better shape. We’re lucky to have met a great neighbour - he’s got cattle on our land, and was kind enough to offer to let us stay in his cottage across the road while we’re renovating our place. So Pete and I moved in there last week, making it a lot easier to do things around the house before/after work. This is a huge change for us from London, but we’re loving it! Short-term we’re getting this house liveable but longer term we’re aiming to build the ‘dream home’ a bit further up the hill. There’s SO much to do, but we took a little break to collate some of the photos from our adventures over the last couple of months…
